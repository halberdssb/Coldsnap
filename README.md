## Summary
Coldsnap is a third-person, action roguelike. Players pilot a robot that has reactivated on a lonely and desolate planet known as Cadmus, home to the largest mining operations in the galaxy. Due to these mining operations, Cadmus has gone cold, and the core of the planet is all but dead. It is the robot’s duty to restart the planet’s frozen center and fight through the layers of the crust to save its dying home.

## Specifications
- OS: Windows 11 x64
- Processor: Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600
- Memory: 16 GB RAM
- Graphics: NVIDIA® GeForce® GTX 1070 Ti or AMD Radeon™ RX 5700
- DirectX: Version 12
- Storage: 10 GB available space
- Sound Card: Windows Compatible Audio Device.
